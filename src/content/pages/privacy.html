Click & Mortar

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026.

This policy describes information collected through the Click & Mortar website and how it is used to respond to inquiries.

Information you share

Our visibility-check forms ask for your name, email address, business name, an optional phone number, and a website link, Google listing, or message. We also record which form page you used. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

How inquiries are handled

We use your details to respond, review your business's public online presence, prepare your requested visibility check, and discuss relevant services. Requesting a check does not sign you up for a newsletter.

Netlify hosts the website and processes form submissions. Our configured Airtable integration can copy inquiry details into our lead-management system to organize follow-up and visibility checks. Information shared with these providers is subject to their own privacy practices: Netlify Privacy Policy and Airtable Privacy Policy.

Website analytics and cookies

This website includes Google Analytics to help us understand visits and improve the site. When it runs, Google Analytics may use cookies and collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate location, and browser or device details. We do not send your form fields to Google Analytics through this site's analytics code.

You can manage cookies in your browser and use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Hosting providers may also process technical request data to operate and protect the website.

Keeping and removing information

We keep inquiry details as needed for follow-up, service delivery, and relevant business records. You can ask us to correct or delete your inquiry details, or stop contacting you, using the contact information below. Some records may need to be retained for ongoing services or required recordkeeping.

Contact

Policy updates

If our website's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
