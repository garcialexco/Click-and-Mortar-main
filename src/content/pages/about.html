About Click & Mortar

A Practical Website Partner for Small Businesses

I'm Alex Garcia, the founder of Click & Mortar in Grand Junction, Colorado. My background is in website design, front-end development, and making digital experiences easier to use.

I built Click & Mortar for business owners who need their website to look professional and work properly, but don't want to spend their days tracking down developers or wrestling with updates.

Some businesses need a new website. Others just need someone dependable to keep the one they have up to date. I focus on clear scope, useful work, and communication you don't need a technical dictionary to understand.

  • Website design and front-end development
  • Monthly maintenance and practical updates
  • Clear expectations and honest recommendations
  • Locally based, with support available remotely
