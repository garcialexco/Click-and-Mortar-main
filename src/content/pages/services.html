How Website Partner Works

No endless tickets. No surprise scope.

Your business owns its domain, hosting, accounts, and website. We manage agreed tasks on sites we can safely support. The goal is simple: a current, functional website and a dependable point of contact.

01 · Assess

Check Your Website

We review your current platform, visible problems, support needs, and who controls the accounts. A free public check comes first; a technical intake follows before paid work.

02 · Onboard

Agree on the Scope

If the site is a fit, we confirm access and set up checks. Healthy supported sites may qualify for $350 onboarding; substantial initial repairs are quoted separately.

03 · Maintain

Keep It Running & Current

We check site health and the contact journey, handle up to an hour of approved small updates each month, and test changes before publishing. Unused update time doesn't roll over.

04 · Inform

Know What's Been Done

You receive a monthly summary listing checks, changes, open issues, and recommendations. Regular requests get an acknowledgment target of two business days.
