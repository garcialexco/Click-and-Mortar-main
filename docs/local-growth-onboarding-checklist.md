# Click & Mortar — Onboarding and First 30 Days Checklist

## Sales and approval
- [ ] Business fits one-location, eligible GBP founding offer.
- [ ] Diagnostic prepared with verified facts, up to three priorities.
- [ ] Owner understands $399/month founding pricing and separately billed extras.
- [ ] Proposal/SOW lists exact work, price, exclusions, billing, cancellation and consent.
- [ ] Written service agreement signed (legal-reviewed wording).
- [ ] First payment received; next billing date recorded.
- [ ] Client entered in CRM with follow-up and reporting dates.

## Business facts
- [ ] Owner contact and preferred communication channel confirmed.
- [ ] Public business name, phone, website, categories, actual opening hours, and legitimate address/service area verified by owner.
- [ ] Services, profitable priorities, regions, service capacity, and key conversion target documented.
- [ ] Current suppliers and conflicts/ownership verified.

## Access and safeguarding
- [ ] GBP **manager invitation** accepted; client retains primary ownership.
- [ ] Website change authority and restricted access confirmed if appropriate.
- [ ] Google performance/analytics access (only as needed) granted safely.
- [ ] No shared passwords, fabricated offices, keyword-stuffed names, fake reviews or unsafe edits.
- [ ] Website lead privacy and consent considerations documented.

## Baseline and execution
- [ ] Date-stamped baseline (GBP interactions/reviews/site contact/listings).
- [ ] Three priorities agreed with client.
- [ ] Safe profile edit list approved.
- [ ] Honest Google review link and QR tested.
- [ ] Mobile contact/form path tested.
- [ ] Three priority directories reviewed; up to two supported fixes submitted.
- [ ] Review reply workflow and approvals agreed.
- [ ] First monthly report drafted and sent with 1–3 next priorities.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Each promised item complete or clearly documented as pending/blocked.
- [ ] Hours/cost recorded against $399 revenue.
- [ ] No invented improvements or unsupported marketing claims.
- [ ] Next check-in and renewal/payment action logged.
